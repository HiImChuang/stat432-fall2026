Cross-validation estimates a model's prediction error (for example, MSE in a regression problem), and repeating the procedure with different folds can show how much that estimate varies. Random forests use out-of-bag (OOB) predictions to estimate prediction error without a separate cross-validation step. How can we quantify the stability of a random forest's OOB error estimate?
