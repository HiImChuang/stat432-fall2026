Understanding the Lagrangian is important to understanding how $\beta$ and $\beta_0$ are chosen given a dataset. Explain briefly why minimizing $g(\theta)$ subject to $h_i(\theta) \leq 0$, for $i = 1, \ldots, n$, can be written as

$$
\min_{\theta}\max_{\alpha \geq 0} L(\theta,\alpha),
\qquad
L(\theta,\alpha) = g(\theta) + \sum_{i=1}^{n}\alpha_i h_i(\theta).
$$
